#### Trajectory Optimization with Neural SDFs

*TUM, 2024*

- Integrated neural signed distance fields (SDFs) into optimal-control trajectory optimization for collision-free planning in complex scenes.
- Built a Neural Network-SDF training and evaluation pipeline, integrated with CasADi/IPOPT (multiple shooting, interior-point) for an end-to-end solver.
- Applied RRT for warm starts; achieved smooth, feasible trajectories under non-convex obstacles and complex dynamics (unicycle/Ackermann), matching classical SDF baselines.

#### Tactic Portfolio Allocation Agent

*TUM, 2024*

- Modelled portfolio allocation as a Markov Decision Process and applied the A2C reinforcement learning algorithm.
- Integrated with the FinRL library, extending it with commission fees and a risk-free asset for a more realistic trading environment.

#### Predicting Yelp's Elite

*UCL, 2023*

- Applied logistic regression and random forest to predict Elite membership in Yelp.
- Addressed class imbalance with resampling techniques; applied PCA for dimensionality reduction.

#### Twitter Trust Index

*DLT Science Foundation / Hedera, 2023*

- Defined and measured cryptocurrency community trust on Twitter using sentiment analysis, bot detection, and clustering.
- Applied topic modelling to the Twitter dataset; identified causal links between the trust index time series and cryptocurrency breaking news.
